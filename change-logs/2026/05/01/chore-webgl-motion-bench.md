Added `webgl-motion-test.html`, a standalone WebGL2 bench page that compares two consecutive camera frames inside a fixed 400×500 ROI and reports the motion ratio as a single readPixels(1×1). Each pipeline stage (texImage2D upload, diff draw, generateMipmap reduce, readPixels) is timed separately with performance.now(); when EXT_disjoint_timer_query_webgl2 is available the GPU-only time is also reported in milliseconds (nanosecond precision, asynchronous). The page is not wired into the production app — it exists only to produce numbers for the detector.js redesign.
