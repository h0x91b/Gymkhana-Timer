Fixed webgl-detector.js stillness reading ~0.58 in a static scene and dropping to ~40 FPS in app-webgl.html. The vertex shader had no explicit attribute layout, so the GLSL linker was free to assign aPos and aUv in either order; on the user's hardware UV ended up receiving the position bytes (clip-space [-1..1]), producing garbage texture sampling that read as a stable ~50% diff between frames. Pinning layout(location=0) on aPos and layout(location=1) on aUv aligns the JS-side vertexAttribPointer setup with the shader. Also switched the camera-frame and FBO textures to NEAREST filtering (only the mipmapped motion target keeps LINEAR_MIPMAP_NEAREST) so the ROI crop pass doesn't blend across pixel edges and inflate stillness with phantom diffs. Added per-frame caching keyed by video.currentTime: app.js calls process() and refreshReferenceSafely() back-to-back in some states, and the previous version re-uploaded, re-cropped, and re-readPixels'd twice; now upload, crop, motion ratio and stillness ratio are each computed at most once per video frame, halving the GPU sync stalls.
